Surveyed elevations replace the USGS lidar wherever the survey covers. Accuracy is the survey's own: the app interpolates between shots and cannot add detail that wasn't measured.

How it works

  1. Search for a farm or zoom in on the map.
  2. Pick a tool, then click points on the map. Double-click or press Enter to finish.
  3. Drag the white handles to adjust. Results update as you go.

Keys: P profile, F field, D drainage area, Backspace undo point, Esc cancel.
