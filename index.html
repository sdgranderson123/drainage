Survey & design files

Existing-ground surveys replace the USGS lidar where they cover. A proposed (FG) surface becomes your design for cut/fill, drainage and storms. CAD linework is drawn on the map. Accuracy is the file's own: elevations are interpolated between shots.

Automatic tries every US state plane, UTM and Iowa regional system and keeps the ones that put your data inside their own area, ranked by the drawing notes, .prj files in a scanned folder, state/county and where the map is. You can pick another from the list it shows. Civil 3D surfaces: export LandXML (Output → Export to LandXML).

Chrome/Edge will ask “Upload N files to this site?”. Choose Upload: the files are only read in this browser tab and are not sent anywhere. Large network folders can take a minute to list.

    How it works

    1. Search for a farm or zoom in on the map.
    2. Pick a tool, then click points on the map. Double-click or press Enter to finish.
    3. Drag the white handles to adjust. Results update as you go.

    Keys: P profile, F field, D drainage area, Backspace undo point, Esc cancel.
